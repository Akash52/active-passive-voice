Quick Reference

Active Subject + Verb + Object → Passive Object + be + Past Participle (+ by Subject)

In active voice, the subject performs the action. In passive voice, the subject receives it. The verb changes to be + V3 (past participle). The doer may appear after "by" or be dropped when obvious.
